NATALY DERAS PORTFOLIO

Open portafolio_nataly_mejorado.html in a browser. The About me button opens
about.html. Both pages can also be served by any static file server.
No installation or build step is required. Google Fonts use an internet
connection; system fonts are used as fallbacks.

ADDING YOUR OWN PORTRAIT
The opening of about.html reserves an empty portrait area at exactly 3:4.
No personal photo has been fabricated or substituted.

1. Add your portrait as assets/portrait.jpg (for example, 900 x 1200 pixels).
2. In about.html, replace the entire div.portrait-placeholder element with:
   <img class="about-portrait-photo" src="assets/portrait.jpg"
        alt="Nataly Deras" width="900" height="1200">
3. Keep the surrounding .portrait-media, photo corners and figure caption.
   The supplied CSS fills the 3:4 area without changing its proportions.

MOTION AND ACCESSIBILITY
The About page has a Pause animation control. The homepage has no pause
button. The system's reduced-motion preference is respected on both pages. Canvas animation stops outside the visible hero
and when the browser tab is hidden. The navbar hides on downward scroll
and returns on upward scroll, with safeguards for mobile menus and keyboard
navigation. Page content remains visible without JavaScript.

CONTENT
